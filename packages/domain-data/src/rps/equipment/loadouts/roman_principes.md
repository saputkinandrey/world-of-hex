# Roman principes: mail variant

Source: [Polybius, Histories 6.23](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Polybius/6%2A.html).
Principes use the same equipment pattern as hastati. The selected build carries
two pila, a Spanish sword represented by `gladius`, and the Republican scutum.
It wears a bronze helmet with feather decoration, one left greave, tunica,
caligae, and `lorica_hamata` instead of the small pectorale.

Mail is a wealth-dependent option in Polybius, not an automatic entitlement of
every princeps. It is selected here to distinguish this prototype build from the
pectorale-equipped hastatus. The two pila still use one shared item definition;
heavy and light variants are not yet separate. The left-greave reconstruction and
helmet representation follow the documented choices in `roman_hastati.md`.

Initially one pilum and the shield are held. The other pilum and sword are carried.
Original arena catalogs, class lenses, and preset definitions remain unchanged.
