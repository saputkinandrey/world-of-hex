# Roman triarii: spear and musculata variant

Source: [Polybius, Histories 6.23](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Polybius/6%2A.html).
Polybius describes triarii as similarly equipped to the other heavy infantry,
but with long spears instead of pila. This preset holds `hasta_triarii` in the
main hand and the Republican scutum in the off hand, with a gladius carried.
It includes no pila.

Worn equipment is a bronze helmet with feather decoration, `bronze_musculata`,
one left greave, tunica, and caligae. The original mail variant has been replaced
at the user's request with a complete bronze muscle cuirass: front and back plates
covering the torso, rather than a small front pectorale. This is the current
prototype build choice, not a claim that Polybius prescribes musculata for triarii.
The new item initially inherits DR, weight, and cost from the existing bronze
cuirass definition and can be tuned independently. It does not protect the limbs.
The spear uses its existing
one-hand catalog representation. The single left greave and helmet follow the
reconstruction choices documented in `roman_hastati.md`.

This preset uses a scutum to follow the shared heavy-infantry equipment pattern
described by Polybius. The older arena build's round shield and bronze cuirass
remain available and unchanged; this independent preset does not replace them.
